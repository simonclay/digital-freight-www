Sea Freight

FCL and LCL on all major trade lanes, with consolidation and bonded storage for staged rollouts, and full track and trace from origin to arrival port.

For larger, heavier and non-urgent shipments, our international ocean freight services provide the perfect solution. Whether it’s a full-scale factory move or a few non-urgent parts and machinery to be packed and shipped worldwide by container, we know what it takes to deliver a service that meets your exact needs. Tell us the details and we’ll tailor a cost-effective route.

We provide full sea export and import services including FCL (Full Container Load) and LCL (Less Container Load), door-to-door or to arrival port, with a particular focus on the Far East, the Americas and the Middle East.

FCL & LCL

A full container to yourself, or consolidated LCL space when the shipment doesn't need one; either way, door-to-door or to arrival port.

Consolidation & bonded storage

Stage a rollout across sites without paying for a container per destination.

Full track & trace

Live visibility and proof of delivery on every booking, in your customer portal.
