Lighting and Audio

Fragile, high-value lighting rigs and audio equipment moved by road, air or sea, handled with the care and track-and-trace visibility this kit demands.

We transport lighting equipment by road to most destinations throughout the UK, mainland Europe and road-accessible areas of Asia, and by air or sea to virtually any global destination. We understand how vulnerable (and valuable) this equipment can be, so it’s always handled with the highest levels of attention and care.

The same applies to audio equipment moving alongside it: our worldwide service includes track and trace and proof of delivery on every leg, so nothing goes quiet between collection and the venue.
