IOR and EOR

Ship into countries where you have no legal entity

Customs authorities need a named party on the declaration. Where you do not have one locally, we stand in as importer or exporter of record and carry the compliance with it.

Importer of Record

We import on your behalf

We take legal responsibility for the import: classification, valuation, duty and VAT, permits and any product approvals the destination asks for. Your equipment clears in our name and is delivered to your site or your customer.

Exporter of Record

We export on your behalf

Filing the export declaration, screening against sanctions and dual-use controls, and holding the licences. Useful when hardware is leaving a country your business is not registered in, including returns and RMA moves.
