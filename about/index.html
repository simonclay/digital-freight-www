About us

Big consignments? We'll give you a big service to match.

Founded in 2009 on a simple idea: specialise in the large, oversize and heavy consignments that most forwarders would rather not deal with, and treat every client like the relationship matters.

When we set up Digital Freight in 2009, comparatively few companies specialised in handling large, oversize and heavy consignments for international destinations.

We deliberately focused on this sector of the marketplace, where we knew that reliability, value for money and quality of service count. Our refreshingly personal and straightforward approach really hit the mark, and this market is where we still have the majority of our clients today.

That personal approach sets us apart. So while, as our name suggests, we embrace the very latest technology, we never regard it as a substitute for contact with another human being.

Much of our success is due to the fact that we only recruit people who genuinely want to provide outstanding customer service. And because we are still owned and operated hands-on by one of the founders of our business, the values we were built on continue to underpin everything we do.

We strive to make our services as uncomplicated and efficient as possible: from placing your order with a knowledgeable operator (or by email if you prefer), through convenient online track and trace, to receiving proof of delivery by phone, email or online.

Our ethos

Expertise and knowledge, with service that stands out

Consistency, reliability, flexibility and a can-do attitude: no challenge or requirement is too much trouble for our customer-focused team. Understanding each client's unique needs lets us work closely with them, acting as an extension of their own business.

The Digital Alliance

Over the years, we've built trusted relationships with overseas agents across Europe, the USA and the Far East: what we call the Digital Alliance. We've researched every major city and handpicked the very best agents in those locations, all following the same ethos of service, reliability, attention to detail and honesty.

Wherever in the world Digital Freight is responsible for your freight, it's handled with the same due care and attention as it would be in our own hands in the UK.
