About us

Specialist logistics for the technology sector. Expertly managed by people who care.

Digital Freight helps businesses simplify complex international logistics through a combination of freight forwarding expertise, Importer of Record (IOR) and Exporter of Record (EOR) services, and advanced shipment visibility technology.

We provide end-to-end logistics solutions that remove the complexity from moving goods around the world. Whether supporting a global technology rollout, delivering critical equipment to a remote location or managing international supply chain requirements, our team handles every stage of the process - from customs compliance and documentation through to final delivery.

Our specialist IOR and EOR services enable businesses to import and export goods compliantly, even when they don't have a local legal entity in the destination country. This allows our customers to access new markets faster and overcome regulatory challenges with confidence.

Technology sits at the heart of everything we do. Through our real-time Track & Trace platform, customers have full visibility of their shipments throughout the journey, providing proactive updates, greater transparency and a full paper trail all in one place. Combined with dedicated and responsive account management we make domestic and international freight management as straightforward and stress-free as possible.

That's why organisations around the world have trusted us to deliver their freight, whilst benefitting from a better customer experience since 2009.
